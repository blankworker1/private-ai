# Campaign — Pre-launch Awareness Series

**Status: concept.** Episodes on the problem can start before launch; anything pointing to the product waits for the Phase 1 trial (see `DECISIONS.md`, Go-to-market).

**Core message:** private AI is not centralised AI.

---

## The idea

Most "made for memes" AI music projects use the same format: an attractive AI character with a message, and the real makers hidden. Astra Blue claims a "Super Intelligence" directed her. Danny Bones' makers stayed anonymous until journalists named them.

private-ai uses the same format but reverses its one dishonest move: **the character shows its masthead.** Every episode ends on a credits card saying who made it, with which tools and models, and where it ran: cloud or local.

The first season is made in the cloud, and says so. Later episodes are made on the Brain PC, and the difference between the two credits cards is the story: from someone else's story to our own.

## Rollout

| Phase | Content | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Character launch | 2–3 episodes on the problem: whose story? the cloud, no masthead | Attention; introduce the character and the credits card |
| 2. The trial | The Brain PC arrives; real household setup footage | Trust, through proof |
| 3. The four levers | One episode each: model choice, Library, instructions, corrections | Show how private-ai differs in practice |
| 4. Launch | Website live; the character pulls its own plug: "Lights off. Your choice." | Point people to private-ai.tech |

---

## Rules for the character

- Shows its credits card in every episode. No exceptions.
- Labelled as AI-generated on every platform.
- Domestic, not glamorous: a household steward, not a pop star. Its authority comes from being checkable, not from charisma.
- Never claims to speak for a movement, a group or "the people".
- Always points back to private-ai.tech and this repo, never only to a platform.
